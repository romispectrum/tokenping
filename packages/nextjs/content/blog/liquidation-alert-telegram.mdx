## Do you want an alert on your loan, or a liquidation feed?

Two very different things are called a liquidation alert on Telegram.

- **An alert on your own loan.** You borrow on Aave or Morpho, and you want a message before your position can be liquidated. The bot reads your health factor from the chain and warns you below a level you choose. This guide is about this kind.
- **A market liquidation feed.** Traders follow bots that post liquidations as they happen on an exchange. Binance publishes a public stream of forced liquidations on its futures market, and Hyperliquid liquidates perpetual positions when an account's equity falls below its maintenance margin. Such feeds say nothing about your loan. If you trade futures on an exchange, services such as Cryptocurrency Alerting can alert on your own exchange positions instead.

There are also open-source bots on GitHub that you can run yourself, with your own bot token from Telegram's BotFather. They suit people who are comfortable running a small server; you then depend on that server staying up.

- **Coverage.** Check that the bot covers your protocol, network and market. Aave V4 Spokes and Morpho Midnight are covered by few of them.
- **What it asks for.** A bot that only needs an address cannot move funds. Be wary of any alert bot that asks for a signature on a transaction or a token approval.
- **How it repeats.** Some bots message once when a level is crossed, others repeat while you stay below it. Tokenping repeats every 15 minutes; HF Guard alerts when your risk level changes.

## How do you set up a Telegram liquidation alert with Tokenping?

1. Open the [Aave dashboard](/aave) or the [Morpho dashboard](/morpho) and connect the wallet you borrow with. Looking at your positions needs no payment.
2. Pay for alert access, in USDC on Arbitrum One, and sign in by signing a message with that wallet.
3. In the market you want to watch, choose Telegram and type your Telegram @username. Your account needs a username.
4. Open the Tokenping bot and press Start. The bot replies to confirm the link.
5. Press **Send test** to check that a message arrives.

From then on Tokenping checks every 5 minutes and, while your health factor is below your threshold, sends a message every 15 minutes. Send /stop to the bot to end messages to that chat. [How to set up a health factor alert](/blog/aave-health-factor-alert-setup) covers every step and the other channels.

## What does a Telegram bot learn about you?

Telegram's documentation is clear about this. When you write to a bot, it sees your Telegram user ID, your first name, your username if you have one, and the language setting of your app, plus what you send it. It can also read some public profile details. Bots are run by their developers, not by Telegram.

So a Telegram alert ties your Telegram identity to a wallet address in the bot operator's database. If that matters to you, read the bot's privacy policy first, or use a channel that reveals less. [Private Aave liquidation alerts](/blog/private-aave-liquidation-alerts) compares Signal, Telegram and email on this point.

## Can a Telegram alert fail?

Yes, in a few ordinary ways.

- **You blocked or muted the bot.** Blocking stops all messages, and a muted chat can stay silent overnight. Allow notifications from the bot's chat.
- **You changed your username.** A bot that links by username can lose the link; with Tokenping you type the new name and press Start again.
- **The bot is down or slow.** Most of these services are in beta and promise no uptime, and Telegram limits how fast a bot can send.
- **The price moves between two checks.** Every bot checks on a schedule, so a sharp drop can cross your line and reach 1.00 before the next check. Set the alert where you still have time to act.

For phone settings that hide messages at night, see [why an alert can reach your phone and still not wake you](/blog/aave-alerts-do-not-disturb).

## Telegram or another channel?

Telegram is quick to set up and works on every platform. Its weak points are privacy, since the bot sees your identity, and that it depends on one app. Many borrowers add a second channel, such as email or Signal, so a missed Telegram message is not the only warning. Tokenping lets one alert go to several channels, each in its own language.

If you would rather have a bot repay for you than warn you, see [DeFi Saver alternatives](/blog/defi-saver-alternatives); for Morpho loans in particular, see [Morpho liquidation alerts](/blog/morpho-liquidation-alerts).
