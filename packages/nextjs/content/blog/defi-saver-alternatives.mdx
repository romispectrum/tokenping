## What does DeFi Saver do?

DeFi Saver is a non-custodial app for managing DeFi positions. For a borrower, two parts matter.

- **Automation** acts on your position. Liquidation Protection, added in August 2026, repays part of your debt from your collateral when DeFi Saver's Safety Ratio falls below a fixed minimum per protocol (105% on Aave V3, 110% on Aave V4, 127% on Morpho Blue). Automated Leverage Management repays below a ratio you set and can also boost above one. There are also stop loss, take profit and price-triggered options on some protocols.
- **Notify** only alerts you, in the app, by email, through Blockscan chat or on Telegram, for Aave, Liquity, Maker and Compound positions on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base and Optimism. It works without Automation.

Automation has conditions. The debt must be at least $30,000 on Ethereum mainnet, or $500 on Arbitrum, Optimism and Base for Aave V3, and DeFi Saver built most strategies for one collateral and one debt asset. Positions usually need to sit in a DeFi Saver smart wallet; Aave V3 and Compound V3 also work from an ordinary wallet, while its pages disagree about Aave V4. Each automated transaction costs 0.05% of its size, plus a swap fee of 0.01% to 0.25% and gas, all taken from the position. Morpho Midnight positions can be managed on DeFi Saver, but they have no automation yet.

## Which tools can repay your loan for you?

Automation is the part of DeFi Saver that is hardest to replace, because few tools still do it.

- **Instadapp Protection Automation** repays debt from your collateral when your health factor falls below a trigger you set, until it reaches your target. It covers Aave V3, needs an Instadapp smart account with an added authority, and charges 0.3% to 0.4% per event, with a minimum of $15,000 in collateral on Ethereum. Its guide is undated, and we could not confirm that it is still live.
- **Ava** watches your Aave V3 health factor on Ethereum and Base. In Manual mode it prepares a repayment or a top-up for you to sign. In Auto mode it acts from a smart wallet you fund, under a permission that by default has no spending limit and expires after 365 days, and it checks hourly. Ava charges nothing today and says a fee is being considered.
- **KeeperHub** lets you build workflows. Its documented Aave V3 example checks every 5 minutes and repays below a health factor of 1.2, from a wallet it holds keys for, after a token approval. This suits people who are happy to configure a bot themselves.
- **Morpho pre-liquidation** is not a repayment. You authorise a contract that lets liquidators sell part of your position earlier and in smaller steps than a full liquidation, for an incentive. [Morpho pre-liquidation explained](/blog/morpho-pre-liquidation) covers how it works.
- **Aave V4 Position Managers** could one day offer protection: Aave's docs name automated liquidation protection as a possible use, but on 8 Nov 2026 they list no live protection manager.

Whichever you pick, read what you authorise. A repayment bot can act again each time your ratio drops, and every action costs a fee and sells collateral at that moment's price.

## Which tools only alert you?

An alert costs less, needs no permission over your funds, and leaves the decision with you. The price is that you have to be reachable and able to act.

- **Otomato** reads Aave V3 and Morpho Blue positions from an address you paste, with an app and a Telegram bot. Core monitoring costs nothing; it also alerts on rate spikes, depegs and governance changes.
- **HF Guard** covers the most protocols here, including Aave V4, Morpho Blue, Compound V3 and Solana lenders, on Telegram and a web dashboard. It is in beta, at no charge, and says alerts are best-effort and may be delayed.
- **DeFi Monitor** covers Aave V3, Spark, Morpho Blue and Compound V3 on Ethereum, Arbitrum and Base, on Telegram or Discord, with a tier at no charge for early users and paid plans from $4.99 a month.
- **DeFi Saver Notify** is DeFi Saver's alert part on its own. If you already use DeFi Saver and only want warnings, it is the simplest switch.
- **Browser extensions** such as Aave DeFi Watcher and Aave Monitor warn you on the desktop. They only work while your browser is open.
- **Tokenping** checks your Aave and Morpho positions every 5 minutes and, while a health factor is below your threshold, messages you every 15 minutes on Signal, Telegram or email. It is the only tool in this list with Signal, and it covers Morpho Midnight fixed-rate loans with maturity reminders.

[Aave liquidation alert tools compared](/blog/aave-liquidation-alert-tools) goes deeper on the Aave side, and [Morpho liquidation alerts](/blog/morpho-liquidation-alerts) on the Morpho side.

## Should you automate or get alerts?

| If this sounds like you                                   | Lean towards |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| You often cannot react within an hour, for example asleep | Automation   |
| Your loan is large enough for the minimums                | Automation   |
| You want no contract to have permission over your funds   | Alerts       |
| You hold several collateral or debt assets in one market  | Alerts       |
| You borrow on Morpho Midnight                             | Alerts       |
| You want to decide how to repay: add, repay or sell       | Alerts       |

Some borrowers use both: automation set well below where they want to act, and an alert above it, so they can step in before the bot does. Either way, keep a margin; [what a safe Aave health factor is](/blog/safe-aave-health-factor) helps you choose one.

## Which tools have shut down?

- **Summer.fi** is gone. Its Pro app became view-only on 12 Feb 2026, its automations stopped and had to be recreated on DeFi Saver, and the Summer.fi app is now deactivated.
- **Morpho's Auto-deleverage** has been retired since 13 May 2026: it takes no new positions, and Morpho asks existing users to turn it off.
- **Gelato Web3 Functions**, which many custom bots ran on, ended on 31 Mar 2026 according to KeeperHub's migration guide; Gelato's site no longer lists them.

If a bot you set up a while ago still appears to protect you, check that it still runs.

## When is DeFi Saver still the better choice?

DeFi Saver remains the broadest automation tool: it covers Aave V3 and V4, Morpho, Spark, Compound, Fluid and Maker, has run automation since 2021, and lists an Immunefi bug bounty of up to $350,000. If your debt clears its minimums and you want a bot to repay while you sleep, few alternatives do as much. An alert tool is better if your position is small, uses several assets, sits on Morpho Midnight, or if you would rather not grant any permission.
