## Does the Morpho app warn you before liquidation?

No. The Morpho app shows each borrow position's health factor and liquidation price, and Morpho's help centre tells borrowers to monitor them, but it has no notifications by email, push or chat. Morpho's developer docs leave alerts to the apps built on Morpho, and suggest warning users when the health factor drops below a level such as 1.1.

A Morpho Blue loan can be liquidated as soon as its health factor reaches 1.00, and the first liquidator can repay all of the debt in one go, as [how Morpho liquidation works](/blog/how-morpho-liquidation-works) explains. That makes an early warning more useful than on protocols that liquidate in steps.

Be careful with bots that claim to be Morpho's. Morpho states that it does not run any Telegram bot.

What differs between them matters more on Morpho than on Aave:

- **Midnight.** Fixed-rate loans end at a maturity date, and after it the loan can be liquidated whatever its health factor. Tokenping sends a reminder in the last 7 days and in the last day, then an alert every 15 minutes after maturity while debt remains. [Morpho Midnight fixed-rate loans](/blog/morpho-midnight-fixed-rate-loans) explains the rules.
- **Pre-liquidation.** If you authorised a pre-liquidation contract, part of your position can be sold before the market's LLTV. Tokenping measures your health factor against that earlier line; DeFi Monitor warns about pre-liquidation as well.
- **How low you can set the alert.** High-LLTV markets often run at a health factor of 1.02 to 1.08, so a tool that only lets you alert at 1.1 or above would message you all the time. Tokenping's Morpho threshold goes down to 1.02.

For Aave loans and the wider list of tools, see [Aave liquidation alert tools compared](/blog/aave-liquidation-alert-tools).

## How do you get a Morpho liquidation alert with Tokenping?

1. Open the [Morpho dashboard](/morpho) and connect the wallet you borrow with. It lists your borrow positions in markets the Morpho app lists, with their health factors, and needs no payment.
2. Pay for alert access in USDC on Arbitrum One and sign in by signing a message with that wallet. One plan covers Aave and Morpho.
3. On the position, set a threshold between 1.02 and 10 and choose Signal, Telegram or email, or several.
4. Press **Send test** to check that a message arrives.

Tokenping reads your health factor from the chain with Morpho's own math every 5 minutes and, while it is below your threshold, messages you every 15 minutes. For Telegram setup in detail, see [how to get a liquidation alert on Telegram](/blog/liquidation-alert-telegram).

## Can anything protect a Morpho loan automatically?

A few things can act without you, each with conditions.

- **Pre-liquidation** lets liquidators sell part of your position earlier and in smaller steps, for an incentive, once you authorise a contract for your market. Anyone can create such a contract, and it does not repay with your own funds. [Morpho pre-liquidation explained](/blog/morpho-pre-liquidation) covers the trade-off.
- **DeFi Saver Automation** can repay a Morpho Blue loan from its collateral. Its Liquidation Protection triggers when DeFi Saver's Safety Ratio falls below 127%. It needs a DeFi Saver smart wallet and at least $30,000 of debt on Ethereum or $500 on Base, charges 0.05% per action plus swap fees, and has no automation for Midnight.
- **Morpho's Auto-deleverage** is being retired: since 13 May 2026 it accepts no new positions, and Morpho asks existing users to turn it off.

[DeFi Saver alternatives](/blog/defi-saver-alternatives) compares the automation tools across protocols.

## What is a Morpho liquidation bot?

Usually a bot that liquidates loans, not one that protects yours. Morpho does not liquidate positions itself: anyone can call the liquidation function, and in practice automated bots do it for a profit. Morpho publishes its own open-source liquidation bots for Morpho Blue and Morpho Midnight, and runs a Liquidation App that lists liquidatable borrowers with an estimated profit.

These are tools for liquidators. If your loan appears in one, it is already at risk; an alert is what tells you before that.

## Where can you see Morpho liquidations?

- **Morpho's data dashboard** has a Liquidations section. For the week of 2 Nov 2026 it showed $13,823,805 liquidated across chains and no realised bad debt.
- **Dune** hosts Morpho's own "Morpho Liquidations" dashboard, with liquidations and bad debt per market.
- **DefiLlama** has a Morpho Blue liquidations page, but it needs a subscription.

A "Morpho liquidation heatmap" search mostly finds pages about liquidations of MORPHO token futures on exchanges. Those track traders' margin positions on the MORPHO token, not Morpho loans.

To work out the price at which your own loan is liquidated, the Morpho app shows it for each position, and [the Morpho health factor guide](/blog/morpho-health-factor) shows the formula.
